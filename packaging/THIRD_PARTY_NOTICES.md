## Optional experimental module audio

The audio control code can use locally supplied QDC507 kernel modules and the
MaVo PCM bridge. These third-party binaries and Android Platform Tools are not
included in this package. Import checks fixed hashes but does not establish
redistribution rights or compatibility with other firmware. See
`docs/QDC507_AUDIO_RESEARCH.md` for provenance, hashes and tested scope. Obtain
and use optional dependencies under their respective terms.
